On Supabase, `shared_preload_libraries` is managed by the platform, and projects are not
able to run `ALTER SYSTEM` themselves. If `auto_explain` doesn't show up in
`shared_preload_libraries` for your project, [contact Supabase support](https://supabase.com/dashboard/support/new)
and ask them to add `auto_explain` to your project's preload libraries.

Once that's confirmed, continue to the next step to configure `auto_explain` at the role level.
