## Finding your Supabase connection information

The collector keeps an ongoing connection and we generally recommend connecting it through
the Supavisor **session pooler** (port `5432` on the pooler hostname), rather than the direct connection.
Supabase's direct connection is IPv6-only by default, and reaching it over IPv4 requires paying for the
[IPv4 add-on](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/ipv4-address).

You can find your session pooler
connection information by clicking on the **Get Connected** button in the Supabase dashboard:

<img src={imgSupabaseGetConnected} alt="Supabase dashboard Get Connected button" className="img-polaroid" style={{ width: '520px' }} />
<br/><br/>
Then select the **Session pooler** option under the **Direct Connection String** tab.

<img src={imgSupabaseConnectionDetails} alt="Supabase connection details showing the Session pooler option" className="img-polaroid" style={{ width: '500px' }} />
<br/><br/>
